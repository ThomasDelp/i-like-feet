Alexandre & the Foot Princess

Alexandre — bald, bespectacled, hopelessly in love — must climb to the top and save the Foot Princess. Flying ongles cost him 1 HP out of 3. Lose all three and you lose a life; he has 3, and restarts from his last green checkpoint ledge.

  • ← → or A D — move (he bounces on his own)
  • M — mute · R — restart · P — pause
  • On touch: hold the left or right half of the screen
